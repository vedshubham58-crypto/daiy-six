# Daily Six – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Daily Six ("the app") is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app uses.

## Data the app stores on your phone
Your puzzle progress, streaks, statistics and whether you bought Daily Six Pro are saved only on your device. The developer does not collect or receive this information.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with **Privacy settings** on the home screen.
How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Purchases (Google Play)
Buying Daily Six Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.
Google Play's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
